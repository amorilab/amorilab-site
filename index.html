01 — About

Signal, systems, and a little solder smoke.

I'm the operator behind W3FDE and the builder behind AMORIlab. By day I work as a radio communications technician on P25 and DMR systems, keeping the radios people depend on working when it matters. I'm also the founder and State Director of the Tennessee Amateur Radio Association, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit for amateur radio advocacy and advancement.

AMORIlab is my self-hosted sandbox for both worlds: a place to test and build radio programs that move LMR and amateur radio forward. It's also home to the TN TAC Simplex Node Hub for AllStarLink.
