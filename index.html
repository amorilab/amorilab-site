01 — About

Signal, systems, and a little solder smoke.

I'm the operator behind W3FDE and the builder behind AMORIlab. By day I work as a radio communications technician — installing, programming, and maintaining the two-way radio systems that people depend on when it matters. After hours, I run a homelab that blends IT infrastructure with RF experimentation.

AMORIlab is my design studio for both worlds: virtualization clusters, segmented networks, monitoring stacks, and radio gateways that link the internet to the airwaves. Every build is documented, reproducible, and designed to be reliable — because that's what the job taught me.

Observability Stack
